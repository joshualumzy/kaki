# Nanyang Kopi & Toast - Kopi Pricing

Owner: Mei (Menu & Store Experience Lead) and Wei Ling (Operations Director)
Last updated: 11 November 2026, before opening

## 1. Old and new prices

- At the old shop, kopi used to be $1.30 a cup. The new menu charges $2.40.
- On this rent, a cup of kopi costs us about $1.60 including the rent share. Anything sold at the old price loses money on every cup.
- The old shop's story is on our menu boards, and many morning regulars have come here for years. Uncle Lim at the kopi bar knows them by name.
- About 40 regulars came in most mornings at the old shop.

## 2. Current record

- The nephew's Facebook post about the new prices has been shared a few hundred times since last night, about 300 shares.
- The till can take a regulars' price button in about 10 minutes, but the shop has no agreed rule for who would receive that price.

Still open: what we do for the old regulars without losing money on every cup.
