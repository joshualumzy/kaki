# Supplier & Egg Quality Contingency Guide

Owner: Wei Ling (Operations Director)
Last updated: 10 November 2026

## Primary Supplier: Ah Seng Poultry Farm (Johor)

- Standard delivery: 80 trays of Grade A kampong eggs arrive at 6:30am daily before the 8:00am Raffles Place breakfast rush.
- If the Tuas checkpoint lorry is delayed until 12:00pm noon, the outlet will run out of eggs by 8:35am during a launch promotion.

## Contingency Options

| Option | Cost | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy 60 trays of Seng Shiong supermarket eggs Thursday night | SGD 180 | Keeps Friday 8am launch on schedule; slightly paler yolks than kampong eggs |
| Postpone $1 Kaya Toast launch promo to Monday morning | SGD 250 | Wasted Friday part-time staff shifts and requires rescheduling social media promos |
