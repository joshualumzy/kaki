# Nanyang Kopi & Toast - Seating Trial Notes

Owner: Wei Ling (Operations Director) and Mei (Menu & Store Experience Lead)
Last updated: 10 November 2026

## 1. The room

- The Raffles Place flagship has 28 seats: booths along the wall and tables in the middle.
- There are 10 window-bar stools in the storeroom.
- Most morning customers come alone carrying a hot tray, about 60% of them, and need a table without waiting for a queue to clear.

## 2. What the trial showed

- At the trial, laptop users held 16 of the 28 seats for about two hours on one kopi. Breakfast customers with trays walked out.
- The blocked seats cost us real breakfast sales at the trial, about $420.
- One of the floor staff got shouted at for asking someone to move. Nobody wants to police tables.
- More than half of the laptop users wore lanyards from the offices upstairs, 9 of 16, and two of those offices have asked about bulk orders.

## 3. Systems

- The booth power sockets are on one timer switch in the back room.
- The Wi-Fi can give each receipt a code that lasts 45 minutes.
- No seating rule has been agreed for the breakfast rush.

Still open: how we free seats for breakfast without anyone having to confront customers.
