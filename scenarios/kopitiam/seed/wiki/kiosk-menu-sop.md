# Nanyang Kopi & Toast - Kiosk Menu & Kitchen Printer Notes

Owner: Mei (Menu & Store Experience Lead) & Arjun (Tech & POS Lead)
Last reviewed: 11 November 2026

## 1. What happened in yesterday's kiosk trial

- Out of 28 first-time expats, tourists, and younger CBD office workers in yesterday's Raffles Place trial, 19 (68%) froze for over 2 minutes at Step 2 (`Kopi O Kosong`, `Kopi C Siew Dai`, `Teh Halia`), backing the morning queue out the glass doors.
- 72% of confused first-timers never noticed the small top-right language toggle before tapping a drink button.

## 2. Kitchen printer and screen limits

- **Kitchen thermal docket:** The docket currently prints the same button name the customer sees. Our 65-year-old kopi uncles read traditional shorthand (`Kopi O Kosong`) at a glance. When we tested English names (`Black Coffee No Sugar`) at Tampines, drink prep slowed by 15 seconds per cup, and local regulars mocked a rival shop on Reddit for calling Kopi O a "Local Americano".
- **POS v2.4:** It can hold a separate kitchen print name from the customer-facing button name, but it has to be configured and kitchen-tested before the 8am rush.
- **15-inch touchscreen grid:** Each page fits 12 buttons (about 18 characters per line). Adding two-line subtitles under all 24 drinks pushes half the menu onto Page 2.

## 3. Still open
