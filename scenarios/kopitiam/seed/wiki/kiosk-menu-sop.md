# Nanyang Kopi & Toast - Kiosk & Menu SOP

Owner: Mei (Menu & Store Experience Lead)
Last reviewed: 28 October 2026

## Ordering Flow

1. Choose Set Meal (Set A: Kaya Butter Toast + 2 Soft-Boiled Eggs + Hot Drink) or Ala Carte.
2. Customize drink sweetness and milk in Step 2.
3. Pay via PayNow QR or card and collect at the counter when ticket number is called.

## Kopi Lingo vs Plain English Trial

- At Raffles Place, 68% of tourists and younger office workers (19 of 28 first-time kiosk users) stalled at Step 2 because the buttons only show traditional terms (`Kopi O Kosong Peng`, `Kopi C Siew Dai`, `Teh Gao`).
- When we replaced the buttons with plain English (`Iced Black Coffee No Sugar`), regular morning uncles complained that it looked like an overpriced cafe and took too long to scan.
- Proposed solution: Keep traditional Kopi names by default, add a top-right **"Kopi Lingo / Simple English" toggle button** with drink icons (3 hours engineering time), and add a one-tap **"Usual Set A"** button on the home screen (20-minute config change).
