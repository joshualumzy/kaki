# Indie Arcade SG - Booth Rules

Owner: Daniel (Marketing Lead)
Last updated: 30 October 2026

Indie Arcade SG runs the showcase floor at the convention. These are the notes from the
exhibitor pack we received when we signed up.

## Allocation

- Booth: 3m x 3m
- One table (1.8m), two chairs and one power point are provided.
- Bring your own extension cord and a power strip with surge protection.

## Setup and teardown

- Setup: 26 November 2026, 4pm-7pm.
- Doors: 27-29 November 2026.
- Teardown: 29 November 2026 after 6pm.

## Demo rules

- No food or drink on the demo tables.
- Keep audio at a reasonable level; headphones are allowed.
- Hand out stickers only, no flyers.

## Contact

Questions go to the organiser inbox. Booth layout changes are sent by email.
