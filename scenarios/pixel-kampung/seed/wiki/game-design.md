# Hawker Heroes - Game Design Doc

Owner: Mei (Game Designer)
Last reviewed: 28 October 2026

## Pitch

Hawker Heroes is a one-thumb mobile arcade game about a young hawker trying to keep a
stall running through the lunch rush. Players swipe to serve orders, chain combos and
upgrade the stall between rounds.

## Core loop

1. Take an order from the queue.
2. Cook and plate it before the patience timer runs out.
3. Bank tips to upgrade equipment.
4. Survive the lunch rush and move to the next neighbourhood.

## Level structure

- 10 levels across three neighbourhoods: Kampung Corner, Night Market and Marina.
- Levels 1-4 teach the order queue, combos and equipment upgrades.
- Level 5 ("The Lunch Rush") is the first real wall: three queues at once and a boss
  order that needs a perfect combo chain.
- Levels 6-10 open up the upgrade tree and finish with the hawker centre showdown.

## Design intent for level 5

The difficulty spike is deliberate. Players should feel the game tighten before the
finale, and the boss order is supposed to be the moment where the combo system clicks.
Tuning is still open while playtest feedback comes in.

## Open questions

- Is the checkpoint before the level 5 boss too far from the start?
- Do we show a one-time hint after two failed boss attempts?
