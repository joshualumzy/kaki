# Lunch spots near the studio

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The official studio lunch rotation, in order of how far we are willing to walk.

1. Kampung Gluttony Food Court - chicken rice, curry puff, kopi.
2. The corner coffeeshop - mee pok and toast; fastest if we leave before noon.
3. Golden Spoon Vegetarian - good on Mondays when the coffeeshop queue is silly.
4. The new sandwich place - expensive, but the deck has aircon.

Lunch is at 12.30pm unless there is a build. Do not schedule meetings over lunch.
