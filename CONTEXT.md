# Domain Context: Kaki Meeting Assistant

## Glossary & Core Concepts

- **`kaki.host`**: The meeting-level agent in `server/src/host/`. It runs the agenda clock, keeps the meeting record through the notetaker, reads follow-ups from the transcript, dispatches them to the right role and triggers the closing minutes. It has no voice of its own: it publishes its cues to the shared screen, and the facilitator-seat AI attendee (Sam in Kopitiam) voices the welcome and hand-overs. Where a pack lets a person take that seat, they speak for themselves.
- **`kaki.wingman`**: The private, role-aware assistant in the meeting dock of the one web app, on a phone or a laptop. It sends silent nudges, answers questions from company records with a source and date, and shows the task cards dispatched to that seat.
- **`kaki.action`**: The background agent in `server/src/action/` that runs an approved task. A suggested task is dispatched to its assignee, and the agent drafts the artifact; after the assignee approves, it writes through one of five connectors: Gitea issues, Mattermost posts, Mailpit email, Wiki.js pages or Radicale calendar events.
- **Owner**: The signed-in host account that plans the meeting and opens the room. The owner edits the setup, shares the invite code and runs the shared screen.
- **Seat**: A place at the meeting, named by the role that fills it. A person or an AI attendee holds it; the join form lists the seats a person can take.
- **Role card**: The briefing a role carries in a scenario (`RoleCard` in `shared/src/scenarioTypes.ts`): who the person is, the facts they know from their side of the company, and what they are answerable for.
- **Team meeting vs Practice meeting**: A team meeting is opened by the signed-in owner, and people join with a meeting code. A practice meeting (`/practice` and `/demo`) needs no sign-in: AI colleagues fill the other seats, and one person speaks or types.
- **Meeting code**: The six-character code, drawn from an alphabet without look-alikes, that admits a person to a team meeting and to LiveKit. It rotates when the meeting changes unless `KAKI_MEETING_CODE` pins it.
- **Nudge pace**: The three timings the owner sets before the meeting: how long into an item the quiet check runs (`quietCheckAfter`), how little an owner must have spoken to count as quiet (`ownerQuietBelow`), and how long before the current item ends the next owner is warned (`comingUpBefore`).
- **Meeting Record**: The server's live snapshot of the meeting, sent as `MEETING_RECORD_UPDATED`: the current agenda index and elapsed seconds, open questions, talk time per role, quiet roles and any judgements the notetaker could not make. Tasks are held in the task store.
- **Minutes card**: The end-of-meeting task "Publish meeting minutes to the team wiki". It carries the Markdown minutes already drafted, is assigned to a person, and writes to Wiki.js only after that person approves.
- **Shared Action Panel**: The shared task list in the meeting dock. It receives every task change for the room; each role's dock receives only the cards assigned to that role.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Dispatch**: The follow-up path from transcript to external write: the notetaker suggests a task, the host dispatches it to the assignee, the assignee accepts it and approves the draft, and `kaki.action` runs it and syncs the result back to the shared task list.
- **Task store**: The server's single owner of task state (`server/src/meeting/tasks.ts`). Every task change goes through it, it enforces the legal lifecycle steps, and it publishes `TASK_UPDATED` to the shared screen and to the assignee's seat.
- **Dispatch**: Moving a suggested task to a named assignee (`SUGGESTED → DISPATCHED`). The host does it automatically when an AI holds the facilitator seat; a person hosting dispatches from the shared task list.
- **Draft approval**: The step where the assignee reviews and can edit the drafted issue, post, email, page or event in their dock and approves it (`DRAFT_READY → RUNNING`). Nothing reaches an external system before this.
- **Scenario**: A swappable demo setting in `scenarios/<id>/`: roles, attendees, AI profiles, agenda, seed data and a replay transcript. `SCENARIO` names the folder; the default is `kopitiam`.
- **Nanyang Kopi & Toast**: The shipped demo scenario: a fictional Singapore kopitiam reopening its Raffles Place flagship.
- **Mailpit**: The self-hosted fake email server; Kaki searches its inbox and `kaki.action` sends approved emails through it.
- **LiveKit Demo Environment**: The self-hosted LiveKit server that carries meeting audio. In the local stack it runs in Docker on `ws://localhost:7880`, and the web app joins it with a room token from the Kaki server.
- **AI attendee** (also **NPC**): A scenario attendee played by the NPC conversation (`server/src/npc/`) instead of a person. Each has a profile in the scenario's `npcs`: voice, personality, agenda, concerns and facts. One LLM, the **Director**, plays all of them and decides who speaks, one at a time. Run with `npm run npc`.
- **Barge-in**: The person talking over an AI attendee. The runner detects it from voice activity on the person's echo-cancelled microphone track, and stops the model, speech synthesis and sound together. The history keeps only what was actually heard.
- **Start prompt**: The briefing screen over the meeting stage after a visitor joins. The AI attendees are in the room but silent, and anything the visitor says or types is ignored, until they press Start the meeting, which posts `/api/meeting/start`. Then the facilitator-seat AI attendee (Sam in Kopitiam) speaks the welcome and hands over to the first agenda item's owner. Where a pack lets a person take that seat, they open the meeting themselves and no AI welcome is spoken.
- **Speech gate**: Decides whether recognised text is the person, or an AI attendee's own voice coming back through the microphone (`server/src/npc/speechGate.ts`).
